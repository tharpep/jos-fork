Question 1:
No, nothing else needs to be done. The IOPL bits live in EFLAGS, and EFLAGS
is part of the Trapframe that gets saved and restored on every context
switch. When an environment traps into the kernel, the hardware pushes
EFLAGS onto the kernel stack as part of the trap, and _alltraps in
trapentry.S folds it into the Trapframe that ends up in curenv->env_tf.
On the way back out, env_pop_tf uses iret, which pops EFLAGS back into the
register, restoring whatever IOPL that environment had. So once we set
FL_IOPL_3 on the FS environment's initial tf_eflags in env_create, that
setting just rides along through every trap and switch with no extra work.
Other environments, which never had IOPL set, similarly keep IOPL=0 across
switches for the same reason.
